Reduce the noise. Amplify the signal. Hand the tools back to the individual.

What we believe

  • Individuals should own their data, their files, their knowledge. Not lease it.

  • Tools should serve one person well, not optimise for a market segment.

  • Knowledge infrastructure — storage, notes, workflow — should be portable and local-first wherever possible, not locked inside someone else's platform.

  • Independence from large platforms is a feature, not a constraint to design around.

  • Self-reliance becomes realistic when the tools actually fit the person using them.

  • A good tool fades into the work. A bad tool inserts itself, asks for attention, collects telemetry, and reshapes the user around its business model.
